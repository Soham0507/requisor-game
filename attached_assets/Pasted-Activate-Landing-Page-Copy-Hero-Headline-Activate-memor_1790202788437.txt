Game Descriptions
Basketball Game
Fast, gesture-controlled hoops that attendees keep coming back to beat their score. A floor-visible leaderboard drives repeat plays, every player registers so you capture the lead, and play metrics show your results.

Cyber Runner
 A cyber-themed endless runner: dodge phishing attacks, block malware, stay ahead of the breach. It turns your product story into gameplay, captures a lead on every run, and gives you engagement data on how visitors play.

Soccer Game
 The same proven engine as our basketball game, reskinned as a fast-paced soccer match. Get a second format for a broader audience, with the same lead capture and metrics, delivered quickly.

Lazer Shooter
 An instant-engagement arcade shooter that pulls a crowd from across the hall. Short rounds keep the line moving, so more visitors play, more leads get captured, and score data shows the traffic it drove to your booth.
