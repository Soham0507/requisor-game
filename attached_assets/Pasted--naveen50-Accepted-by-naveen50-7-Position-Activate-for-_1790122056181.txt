#7 - Position Activate for agencies
What & Why
Refine the Activate landing page so experiential marketing agencies and ad agencies immediately understand RequisorAI as a partner for creating high-impact branded activations—not just a library of booth games. The messaging should take inspiration from Elevation3D's clear experiential-design positioning while using original language grounded in Activate's actual capabilities.

Done looks like
The hero clearly speaks to agencies that need to showcase a brand, attract an audience, and create memorable participation.
The page presents Activate as an experiential design and production partner for branded games, interactive installations, live events, and digital extensions.
Supporting copy explains the agency value: fast-to-understand experiences, strong brand integration, repeat engagement, shareability, and flexible delivery across screens and venues.
Work, capability, partner, and final-call-to-action sections use consistent agency-facing language and give visitors a clear reason to start a conversation.
Copy remains accurate to the current product and does not invent clients, capabilities, performance claims, or case-study results.
Existing Activate branding, game catalog requirements, Coming soon labels/order, and social links remain unchanged.
Out of scope
Visual redesign, layout changes, animation changes, or new media assets.
Changes to game behavior, Customize flows, embedded previews, or production asset paths.
Changes to the existing catalog, game availability, Coming soon ordering, or social destinations.
Creating the separate stakeholder pitch deck covered by Task #4 (Present Requisor.AI clearly to partners and stakeholders).
Steps
Audit the current message hierarchy -- Identify the homepage copy that should be revised first so the agency audience is clear within the hero and reinforced through the rest of the page.
Rewrite the positioning -- Update the hero, manifesto, work introduction, capabilities, partner framing, and CTA language around branded experiential work, audience participation, and memorable brand moments.
Keep proof grounded -- Retain or sharpen existing examples only where they are supported by the current page, and avoid adding unsupported agency or production claims.
Preserve the existing product flow -- Keep navigation, Activate branding, catalog statuses and ordering, social links, and all game interactions intact while validating the updated copy at desktop and mobile widths.
